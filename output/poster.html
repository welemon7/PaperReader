Problem

Existing deep shadow removal methods often suffer from severe artifacts around shadow boundaries and inconsistent illumination because they focus locally and ignore the global contextual correlation between shadow and non-shadow regions.

Core Idea

ShadowFormer is a transformer-based network derived from a Retinex-based shadow model, designed to restore shadow-free images by leveraging global context. It employs a multi-scale encoder-decoder architecture using Channel Attention (CA) modules to efficiently capture hierarchical information. At the bottleneck stage, a Shadow-Interaction Module (SIM) utilizes Shadow-Interaction Attention (SIA) to model the correlation between shadow and non-shadow regions, allowing uncorrupted non-shadow areas to guide the restoration of shadowed areas for better illumination consistency and boundary artifact reduction.

Why It Matters

We introduce a Retinex-based shadow model that formulates the degradation and motivates exploiting global contexts for shadow removal.

Method

ShadowFormer is a transformer-based network derived from a Retinex-based shadow model, designed to restore shadow-free images by leveraging global context. It employs a multi-scale encoder-decoder architecture using Channel Attention (CA) modules to efficiently capture hierarchical information. At the bottleneck stage, a Shadow-Interaction Module (SIM) utilizes Shadow-Interaction Attention (SIA) to model the correlation between shadow and non-shadow regions, allowing uncorrupted non-shadow areas to guide the restoration of shadowed areas for better illumination consistency and boundary artifact reduction.

Key Formulas: - $$ \mathbf{I}_{s} = \mathbf{I}_m \odot \mathbf{L}_{s} \odot \mathbf{R} + (1-\mathbf{I}_m) \odot \mathbf{L}_{ns} \odot \mathbf{R} $$ The Retinex-based shadow model decomposing the shadow image into shadow/non-shadow regions based on illumination and reflectance. - $$ \mathbf{I}_{s}= \mathbf{I}_m \odot \mathbf{I}_{s} + (1-\mathbf{I}_m) \odot \mathbf{I}_{ns} $$ The basic decomposition of a shadow image into shadow and non-shadow regions using a mask. - $$ \text{SIA}(\mathbf{X}, \mathbf{\Sigma}) = \text{softmax}(\frac{\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^T}{d})\mathbf{V} $$ The core attention mechanism in the Shadow-Interaction Module, modified by correlation maps to emphasize context between shadow and non-shadow patches. - $$ \tilde{\mathbf{X}}= \text{SIA}(\text{CA}(\text{LN}(\mathbf{X})),\mathbf{\Sigma})+ \mathbf{X} $$ The residual connection formulation within the Shadow-Interaction Module combining Channel Attention and SIA.

Contributions
  • [theory] We introduce a Retinex-based shadow model that formulates the degradation and motivates exploiting global contexts for shadow removal.
  • [method] We propose ShadowFormer, a lightweight single-stage transformer network built on a multi-scale channel attention framework for end-to-end shadow removal.
  • [method] We design a Shadow-Interaction Module (SIM) with Shadow-Interaction Attention (SIA) in the bottleneck stage to exploit global contextual correlations between shadow and non-shadow regions across spatial and channel dimensions.
  • [application] We achieve state-of-the-art performance on ISTD, ISTD+, and SRD datasets using up to 150x fewer model parameters compared to existing methods.
