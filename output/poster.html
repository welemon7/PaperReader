ShadowFormer: Global Context Helps Image Shadow Removal

Lanqing Guo 1
Problem

Existing deep learning methods for image shadow removal often suffer from severe artifacts around shadow boundaries and inconsistent illumination because they focus locally and ignore the global contextual correlation between shadow and non-shadow regions.

Core Idea

ShadowFormer is a lightweight, single-stage transformer-based network designed for end-to-end image shadow removal. It employs a multi-scale channel attention encoder-decoder architecture to efficiently capture hierarchical global information. At the bottleneck stage, a novel Shadow-Interaction Module (SIM) utilizes Shadow-Interaction Attention (SIA) to model the contextual correlation between shadow and non-shadow regions, leveraging the non-shadow areas to guide the restoration of shadow regions for improved illumination and color consistency.

Why It Matters

We introduce a Retinex-based shadow model, which formulates the degradation of shadow and motivates us to exploit the global contexts for shadow removal.

Key Formulas: - $$ \mathbf{I}_{s} = \mathbf{I}_m \odot \mathbf{L}_{s} \odot \mathbf{R} + (1-\mathbf{I}_m) \odot \mathbf{L}_{ns} \odot \mathbf{R} $$ The Retinex-based shadow model decomposing the shadow image into shadow and non-shadow regions based on illumination and reflectance. - $$ \text{SIA}(\mathbf{X}, \mathbf{\Sigma}) = \text{softmax}(\frac{\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^T}{d})\mathbf{V} $$ The Shadow-Interaction Attention mechanism that reweights attention maps using correlation maps to emphasize context between shadow and non-shadow patches. - $$ \mathcal{L}(\mathbf{I}_{gt},\; \hat{\mathbf{I}})=\left\|\mathbf{I}_{gt}-\hat{\mathbf{I}}\right\| $$ The loss function used to train the network, measuring the difference between the ground truth and the predicted shadow-free image.

Overview of the ShadowFormer network. The channel attention transformer-based encoder and decoder are to extract hierarchical information from the input shadow image, and to reconstruct the shadow-free image, respectively, using a series of channel attention (CA) modules. In the bottleneck stage, we adopt a Shadow-Interaction Module (SIM) to exploit the context information across both spatial and channel dimensions from non-shadow region to help shadow region restoration.
The detailed architectures of ShadowFormer model components. (a) Channel Attention (CA) Module in the encoder and decoder. (b) Shadow-Interaction Module (SIM), as well as an illustration of Shadow-Interaction Attention (SIA). Reweighting the attention map by the correlation map between shadow and non-shadow patches to emphasize the contextual correlation between shadow and non-shadow regions.
