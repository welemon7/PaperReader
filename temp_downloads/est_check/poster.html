Detail-Preserving Latent Diffusion for Stable Shadow Removal

Jiamin Xu\\ Hangzhou Dianzi University\\ superxjm@yeah.net
Motivation

The paper addresses high-quality shadow removal under complex global illumination, where methods trained on limited datasets often overfit and generalize poorly to unseen scenes.
